# About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/), supervised by [Professor Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/). My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**, with specific interests in:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**

I received my B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024.
